# Journal requirements and aesthetic profiles

Source review date: 2026-10-13. Recheck before actual submission. Exact journal, article type, main/supplementary material, initial/final stage, and publisher updates control requirements. Family membership does not establish identical specifications. No automatic compliance certification is provided.

Nature final-submission guidance favors editable, unflattened vector artwork and avoiding rasterized text/line art. SVG is useful for editing but does not automatically meet the accepted upload formats of every journal. Do not apply cover-art or graphical-abstract requirements to data figures.

## Aesthetic starting points (toolkit recommendations, not journal mandates)

- General multidisciplinary: balanced panels, clear lowercase/appropriate panel letters, restrained categories, direct labels.
- Clinical: readable effect sizes and confidence intervals, reference lines, denominators, censoring/risk tables, concise legends.
- Chemistry/materials: clear structures/schemes, calibrated spectra, explicit offsets/baselines/test conditions, comparable performance scales.
- Cell/imaging: acquisition integrity, channel identity, verified scale bars, matched display adjustments, quantitative supporting panels.

## Requirement record for a real submission

Save a task-specific record with journal, article type, stage, official URL, access date, quoted/summary requirement, applicability, and verification status. Record width/height in mm, font size at final size, raster class/DPI, color mode, accepted file formats, file-size limit, panel-letter convention, and image/AI policies only if verified. Unavailable constraints remain unknown; produce an explicit pending-check list instead of fabricated rules.
